# Floating Damage Numbers — From the Depths Mod

A mod that adds floating damage numbers to the game **From the Depths**.
I got inspired by Endless Space 2's scan on fleet battles and Total War: WARHAMMER 3 Damage Numbers mod, and I do enjoy seeing the damage numbers pop up in the game. So I decided to make a mod for it

--

Damage numbers are displayed in 3D world space, above the impacted blocks.
Performance varries depending on the number of blocks being hit, but it should be able to handle a few hundred blocks being hit at once without any issues.

Multiplayer compatibility isn't tested.
Mod may not be compatible that interact with damage models.
There may be inaccuracies in the damage numbers displayed, but so far it's mostly accurate from inital hits.
